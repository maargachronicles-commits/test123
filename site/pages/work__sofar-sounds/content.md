(THE BRIEF)

Sofar had built one of the most intimate global music communities, but its partnerships, visibility, and content strategy were not keeping pace with its growth.

(THE WORK)

We scaled Sofar without losing the soul. From booking talent to designing branded experiences with partners like Xfinity and Acura, the team built integrated music programs that still felt like a living room performance while expanding the reach and cultural relevance.
